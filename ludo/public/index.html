How to play
  • Roll a 6 to bring a pawn out of your yard.
  • Move around the board and into your coloured home lane. You need an exact roll to finish.
  • Land on an opponent to knock them back to their yard. ⭐ Star squares and start squares are safe.
  • Rolling a 6, capturing, or reaching home gives you a bonus roll. Three 6s in a row lose the turn.
  • First to bring all 4 pawns home wins!
  • Grab the dice and flick it to throw (or just tap it). Drag your buddy onto the glowing square (or tap it).
  • Tap any character to poke it. They giggle and dance… and get grumpy if you overdo it 😠
  • Drag the empty board to rotate, pinch or scroll to zoom. 🌙 switches to night mode. On a computer, Space rolls and 1–4 moves a buddy.
