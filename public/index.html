    1. Pick a chip, then tap a symbol to bet. Tap again to add more. You can bet on several symbols.
    2. The host rolls 6 dice.
    3. If your symbol lands on 2 or more dice, you win your bet × that number, and you get your bet back.
    4. If it lands on 0 or 1 dice, the bank keeps your bet.
